## 🎯 AIM

## 📖 INTRODUCTION

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has transformed the way videos are created. Text-to-video generation tools can convert simple textual descriptions into visually appealing videos.

By using Prompt Engineering, we can control the subjects, colors, lighting, background, camera movements, textures, and artistic styles of generated videos.

This experiment explores how different prompting techniques improve the quality and similarity of AI-generated videos.

## ⚙️ PROCEDURE

### Step 1: 🔍 Analyze the Original Video

Carefully observe the given video and identify the following elements:

- Objects/Subjects: People, animals, vehicles, or other objects.
- Colors: Dominant colors and color combinations.
- Textures: Smooth, rough, glossy, or realistic surfaces.
- Lighting: Brightness, shadows, and light effects.
- Background: Indoor, outdoor, or natural surroundings.
- Composition: Camera angle, subject position, and perspective.
- Style: Realistic, cinematic, animated, or artistic.

### Step 2: ✍️ Create the Basic Prompt

Write a simple description of the original video.

Example:

"A beautiful landscape with mountains, trees, and a flowing river."

### Step 3: 🎨 Refine the Prompt with Details

Add more information about colors, lighting, mood, atmosphere, and movements.

Example:

"A beautiful landscape featuring purple mountains, green trees, and a flowing river under a golden sunset sky."

### Step 4: 🖌️ Identify the Visual Style

Specify the artistic style and appearance of the video.

Example:

"A cinematic, ultra-realistic landscape with natural lighting, vibrant colors, and smooth camera movement."

### Step 5: 🔧 Adjust and Fine-Tune

Improve the prompt by including additional details such as:

- Camera movement and angles.
- Weather and environmental effects.
- Object movements and transitions.
- Lighting and shadow effects.
- Video quality and frame consistency.

### Step 6: 🚀 Generate the Video

Enter the refined prompt into an AI video generation tool and generate the video.

### Step 7: 🔄 Compare and Improve

Compare the generated video with the original video based on:

- Subject similarity.
- Color accuracy.
- Background and composition.
- Motion and camera movement.
- Lighting and visual style.

Modify the prompt whenever necessary to achieve better results.
